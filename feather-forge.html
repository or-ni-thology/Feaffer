Structure

the wind it grew against
leading edge narrows — the mark of a flight feather
soft, disordered barbs at the base

Pigment

base & shadow of the barbs
dark bands across the vane
a dark tip, as on a grouse or pheasant

Iridescence

two hues it shifts between
where along the vane it pools

Provenance

a coordinate seeds a specific feather
Every feather here is imaginary and entirely reproducible. The same grid reference always yields the same bird's leaving. ❦
